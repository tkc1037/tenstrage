# Tenstrage Article Writing Rules

## Voice & Tone
- **Primary audience**: Tokyo taxi driver job seekers (career changers, 30-50 years old)
- **Tone**: Professional, credible, practical, and motivating
- **Style**: Clear Japanese with concrete examples and field data

## Content Structure
1. **Opening paragraph**: Hook with specific benefit/data, establish credibility
2. **Main sections (H2)**: 4-6 major sections with clear progression
3. **Subsections (H3)**: Break complex topics into digestible chunks
4. **Evidence**: Include real driver quotes, salary figures, and concrete examples
5. **Closing**: Strong call-to-action linking to job platforms

## Key Requirements
- **Length**: 2,000+ Japanese characters (約1,000+ words)
- **Real experience references**: Include quotes from actual drivers (e.g., "Sさん（50代）は実際に...") or real field insights
- **Affiliate links**: Include [AFFILIATE_LINK] placeholder or format: `https://px.a8.net/svt/ejp?a8mat=TENSTRAGE`
- **Salary data**: Always include concrete figures (月営収, 年収, 歩合率)
- **Practical advice**: Step-by-step guidance, comparison tables, or actionable checklists

## Language & Details
- Use specific numbers and percentages (avoid vague language)
- Include location references (東京23区、六本木、渋谷, etc.)
- Reference 2026 data and trends where applicable
- Use masculine professional Japanese for credibility
