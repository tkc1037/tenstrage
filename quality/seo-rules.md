# Tenstrage SEO Rules

## Keyword Strategy
- **Primary keywords**: タクシードライバー転職, 東京タクシー年収, 年収 800万, 入社祝い金
- **Location keywords**: 東京, 新宿, 渋谷, 六本木, 羽田空港, 東京23区
- **Long-tail keywords**: "年収+具体数字", "エリア別", "未経験から"
- **Intent**: Job seeker conversion - target "転職", "求人", "給与", "年収", "稼ぎ方"

## Title Optimization
- **Length**: 45-60 characters (includes emotional/benefit trigger)
- **Format**: [Benefit/Data] + [Topic] + [Year/Qualifier]
- **Keywords**: Include 1-2 primary keywords naturally
- **Power words**: 完全, 徹底, 実際, 激変, チャンス, 新戦略

## Meta Description
- **Length**: 120-160 characters exactly
- **Format**: [Value proposition] + [Specific data/number] + [Action]
- **Include**: At least one specific figure or percentage
- **CTA**: Subtle call to action ("詳細を確認", "徹底解説")

## Content Optimization
- **H2 headings**: 4-6 per article, keyword-rich but readable
- **H3 headings**: 2-3 per H2 section, answer user questions directly
- **First 100 words**: Dense with keywords and value proposition
- **Keyword density**: 1-2% for primary keyword
- **Internal links**: Link to other relevant articles in site
- **External authority**: Cite real sources (国土交通省, GOジョブ, etc.)

## Freshness & Authority
- **Date targeting**: Include "2026年", "最新", "4月運賃改定" for freshness
- **Data authority**: Use real statistics (有効求人倍率9.11倍, 充足率84.9% etc.)
- **Local authority**: Include Tokyo-specific context (23区, 武蔵野市, 三鷹市)
- **Update cycle**: Refresh seasonal content (梅雨期, 観光シーズン, 年末年始)

## Tag Strategy
- **Required tags**: ["タクシー転職", "東京"]
- **Optional tags**: Add specific topics (年収, 給与, 入社祝い金, GO Crew, エリア別, etc.)
- **Total**: 3-4 tags per article
